## Act 5: Uptown (campus map > Uptown)
Rooms: Uptown street, Gallery Pierre, Club Velour, Cafe Snoot (archway at the far right of the street), Velour backstage (black door left of the club stage).

1. Street: talk to Mimi (stuck in an invisible box; Pierre in the gallery has her key). Talk to Brutus (list or VIP pass only; Pierre has the passes).
2. Gallery: talk to Pierre (he fears "Nothing No. 5" is upside down). Look at the Blank canvas: the tiny signature is upside down.
3. Talk to Pierre: "Nothing No. 5 really is upside down" gets the **VIP pass**. Also ask about the key on the plinth (he wants "greater genius") and "Ask about art" (comeback 1: "A puddle is just an ocean that's practising").
4. Use the Art supplies cart twice: **paint set**, then a **paint smock**.
5. Street: Mimi, "Ask if Pierre is a snob": her beret flies onto Dex's head (**Mimi's spare beret**). Mimi, "Ask what people say about her act" (comeback 2: "Anyone could have. Nobody did").
6. Combine the beret and the smock: **Tortured Artist Disguise**.
7. Give the VIP pass to Brutus (or the Club Velour door) to get in. Brutus, "Ask about the rules" (comeback 3: "Rules are velvet ropes. They only work if you believe in them").
8. Club: talk to Jett Vex (wants to paint Pierre's canvas). Jett, "Ask how she handles critics" (comeback 4: "Noise is just music nobody's invited to dinner yet"). Roxy explains the Finals.
9. Gallery: Pierre, "Jett Vex wants to paint on your blank canvas": he hands over **Nothing No. 5**.
10. Club: give Jett the canvas and the paint set. She paints **Something No. 1**, gives it to Dex, and asks him to show it to the critic Vivian Quill at Cafe Snoot first.
11. Club: go through the Backstage door. Talk to Grizz the roadie (Brutus confiscated his knitting needles). Ask what he's knitting (a scarf for Brutus) and "Ask about touring" (comeback 5: "You haven't seen it all. You've just seen it all so far").
12. Street: Brutus, "Ask about the confiscation bin", then "Tell him what Grizz is knitting": he gives up the **knitting needles**.
13. Backstage: give the needles to Grizz: he gives you Jett's **rider croissant** (the croissant under the dome disappears).
14. Cafe Snoot: Anton the waiter blocks Vivian. Ask Anton about the pigeon (a croissant on the terrace brings down every pigeon uptown).
15. Give the croissant to Mr. Pemberton the pigeon: the flock descends, Anton runs inside, and Vivian is open (Anton and Pemberton disappear).
16. Vivian snubs florals. Use the Tortured Artist Disguise on her.
17. Vivian, "Challenge her to an art debate": the Critique-Off. Answer: noise > "Noise is just music..."; puddle > "A puddle is just an ocean..."; anyone could > "Anyone could have. Nobody did."; real gallery > "Rules are velvet ropes..."; seen it all > "You've just seen it all so far." Any wrong answer: dismissed, retry from the start.
18. Show Vivian Something No. 1: she writes **Vivian's Rave Review** (five stars).
19. Club: talk to Jett (her phone explodes with the review): **guitar pick** (token).
20. Gallery: give Pierre the painting (or the review) with both in hand: **invisible key**. Both are hung on the wall.
21. Street: give the key to Mimi: **white glove** (token).
22. Club: talk to Roxy (or use a token on her) for the Finals. Honest answers win: "I show up with what people need", "everybody's scared of something", "I'm nervous. I'm still doing it anyway." Runs `<<act 6>>`.

Stuck? Use the Jett Vex poster on the street, or ask Roxy for a tip: Dex says what to try next.
