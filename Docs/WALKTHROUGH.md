# Big Night Out: full walkthrough

Use this to test the whole game. Every act's round needs that act's two tokens.

## Act 1: Freshman (dorm, lounge, campus, library)
1. Dex's room: search the bed for a dollar. Read Uncle Vic's letter on the desk.
2. Hallway: dollar in the vending machine for earplugs. Earplugs to Gil for the Hank Hollister album.
3. Drinks table: take the lemon, then the soda; combine them into Hallway Hooch.
4. Album and Hooch to Lacey Rae. She dances and gives you her **cowboy hat**.
5. Lounge: watch the TV (optional), then use the sign-up sheet with the hat and choose Sign up. You get the pen.
6. Hallway stairs to campus. Take a flyer from the info kiosk; combine pen + flyer into a note.
7. Library: Ivy won't talk with Ms. Hush watching. Give her the note.
8. Campus: take the frisbee from the grass and throw it at the founder statue for the sunglasses.
9. Sunglasses to Ivy: she gives you her **bookmark**. Talk to Roxy on the stage for the Talent Round.

## Act 2: Greek Week (campus map > Greek Row)
1. Talk to Tess (sorority porch) about the stolen flamingo Fernanda, then to Chad at the frat door (no toga, no entry).
2. Talk to Tess again and take the bedsheet from the clothesline.
3. Fuse Box bar: talk to Dolly (blown fuse), then ask her for a safety pin. Combine sheet + pin into a toga.
4. Give the toga to Chad. Inside, use the breaker box to get the fuse (lights out), then grab Fernanda.
5. Fernanda to Tess for the **sorority pin**. Fuse to Dolly, request "Disco Volcano" for the **glow bracelet**.
6. Quiz Round with Roxy: Hank Hollister, "Ode to the Quiet Kid in the Back Row", Fernanda, Disco Volcano.

## Act 3: Lab Rats (campus map > Science Hall)
1. Lab: Mina's hamster, Professor Nibbles, is in the vents. Rooftop: Sky's megaphone needs batteries.
2. Ask Mina for spare batteries; give them to Sky. The megaphone scares the pigeon off the bird feeder.
3. Take bird seed (rooftop feeder) and a cardboard tube (lab recycling); combine into a hamster trap.
4. Use the trap on the lab's big vent. Nibbles to Mina for the **safety goggles**.
5. Ask Mina about Nibbles' future for the retirement plan; give it to Sky for the **protest button**.
6. Dare Round with Roxy in the lab: foam volcano (not the blue beaker), lead Sky's chant, hum smooth jazz.

## Act 4: Off Campus (campus map > Downtown)
1. Talk to Bree (needs a camera and a captain's hat), Lou (crossword: sailor's word for the bathroom) and Gus at the Rusty Anchor.
2. Take the fishing net from the junk barrel; use it on the downtown fountain for a quarter.
3. Quarter in the jukebox, play B-7 "The Barnacle Waltz". Ask Gus about Lou's clue: he writes HEAD on a coaster.
4. Coaster to Lou for the **finished crossword** and a disposable camera. Ask Gus for his captain's hat.
5. Hat and camera to Bree, develop the film at the 1-Hour Photo, headshots to Bree for the **diner name tag**.
6. Date Round with Roxy: the midnight classic at the Bijou, fluffy slippers, "Break a leg tomorrow".

## Act 5: Uptown (campus map > Uptown)
1. Mimi is stuck in an invisible box; Pierre at the gallery has her key. Brutus wants a VIP pass.
2. Gallery: the blank canvas's signature is upside down. Tell Pierre: he gives you a VIP pass.
3. Pass to Brutus. In the club, Jett wants to paint on Pierre's blank canvas. Tell Pierre; he gives you the canvas.
4. Take the paint set from the FREE PAINT cart. Canvas and paint to Jett for the **guitar pick** and her painting.
5. Painting to Pierre for the invisible key; key to Mimi for the **white glove**.
6. Finals with Roxy at the club stage: pick the honest answers.

## Finale: Speed Daters Live (campus map > TV studio)
1. Roxy needs a mic, a clean shirt and a dance-off track, and Chet says Dex is cheating.
2. Studio camera: Chet's walkie. Give it to Roxy, then Tess's sign-out sheet (from the audience) to prove it.
3. Wardrobe rack: lapel mic. Mina sends you to Sky for a 9-volt; combine them.
4. Bree gives a napkin and a tip; then Lou gives soda water. Combine them to clean the shirt.
5. Sound board: eject Chet's tape. Give it to the audience: Dolly records Floor Fillers over it. Use it on the sound board.
6. Talk to Roxy for the show: token callbacks, Uncle Vic's motto ("Be the guy who shows up with what they need"), and the dance-off.
