## Act 2: Greek Week (campus map > Greek Row)
1. Greek Row: talk to Tess on the sorority porch. The frat stole Fernanda, the flamingo.
2. Talk to Chad at the frat door: toga party, no toga, no entry.
3. Talk to Tess again, choose "Tell her about the toga rule", then take the bedsheet from the clothesline.
4. Fuse Box bar: talk to Dolly (her fuse is blown), then ask her for a safety pin. Combine the sheet and the pin into a toga.
5. Give the toga to Chad. Inside the frat house, talk to Moose, the pledge guarding Fernanda. Ask about flamingo watch (he only leaves if another pledge relieves him) and about his necktie (he would trade it for food).
6. Use the breaker box to get the 30-amp fuse. The lights go out, but Moose still won't let go of Fernanda.
7. Take the basement door. Talk to Dunk (ask about the dryer and about Mother Whitlock). The blackout stopped the dryer, so take a pledge beanie out of it.
8. Upstairs, give the beanie to Moose. In the dark he thinks you're a new pledge and goes to sleep in the basement.
9. Grab Fernanda, leave through the front door and give her to Tess for the **sorority pin**.
10. Fuse Box: give the fuse to Dolly and request "Disco Volcano". Mother Whitlock, the sorority housemother, confiscated her record. Ask Dolly about the record and about how she lost to Mother Whitlock (comeback: the sugar).
11. Use the bar counter to get a free Volcano Smoothie.
12. Frat basement: give the smoothie to the sleeping Moose for his clip-on necktie.
13. Greek Row: use the necktie on the sorority door to get into the parlor. Read the manners sampler (comeback: the curtains).
14. Talk to Mother Whitlock and take the parlor test. You fail, but now everyone tells you their comeback.
15. Learn the rest of the comebacks: Tess (ask how to survive the test: the shirt) and Moose in the basement (ask if he knows Mother Whitlock: the gloves). Dunk already gave you the one about the house.
16. Take the test again: "A friend with far better taste...", "...all the luckier to be a guest...", "None for me, thank you...", "...allow me to bow instead...", "Forgive me, I was speechless...". Then choose the Disco Volcano record.
17. Give the record to Dolly for the **glow-stick bracelet**.
18. Talk to Roxy and choose Start the Quiz Round: Hank Hollister, "Ode to the Quiet Kid in the Back Row", Fernanda, a necktie, "Disco Volcano".
