# Big Night Out: full walkthrough

Every act's round needs that act's two tokens. After Act 1, new areas open from the map on the campus info kiosk. If you're stuck, each act has a hint: look for "what should I do next" options (the mirror in Act 1, Roxy, or Mr. Bones in Act 3).

## Act 1: Freshman (dorm, Gil's room, lounge, campus, Student Union, library, upper stacks)
1. Dex's room: read Uncle Vic's letter on the desk. Search the bed for a dollar. (The mirror gives hints any time.)
2. Hallway: talk to Lacey Rae (wants a real country record and a drink with zing) and Gil (roommate Boomer snores).
3. Dollar in the vending machine for earplugs. Earplugs to Gil: he lets you into room 216 to take his country album.
4. Room 216: Boomer is sleepwalking in front of the record crate. Take the air freshener (window) and the alarm clock (desk).
5. Talk to Boomer and ask what he's dreaming about: possum waffles from the Student Union.
6. Drinks table: take the lemon. The soda is gone. Stairs down to campus; take the Student Union signpost.
7. Union: talk to Doreen (after nine she only feeds staff and Pokey the Possum) and Kyle (on break in the Pokey suit, it stinks).
8. Give the air freshener to Kyle: he lends you Pokey's head.
9. Use Pokey's head on Doreen: she serves "Pokey" a possum waffle and a soda. Kyle takes his head back.
10. Back in room 216, give the waffle to Boomer: he sleepwalks off to the lounge. Take Hank Hollister Live from the crate.
11. Combine lemon + soda into Hallway Hooch. Give the album and the Hooch to Lacey Rae: one dance, and her **cowboy hat**.
12. Lounge: use the sign-up sheet (or give it the hat) and sign up. You get the pen.
13. Learn comebacks along the way: ask Lacey if she likes poetry ("only one you ever use is 'me'"), ask Doreen what's good (onion soup), ask Kyle about being the mascot (car crashes). Gil's pillow line is a dud.
14. Campus: take a flyer from the info kiosk; combine pen + flyer into a note. Talk to Roxy on the stage.
15. Library: Ivy won't talk with Ms. Hush watching. Give Ivy the note: Percival Thorne stole her notebook and took it up to the stacks.
16. Ask Ms. Hush what's upstairs: she won't let you up the ladder, and gives you the puddle comeback.
17. Use the alarm clock on the card catalogue: it rings, Ms. Hush hunts for it, and the ladder to the stacks is free.
18. Upper stacks: talk to Percival, ask for the notebook (slam rules), then challenge him to a slam-off.
19. Slam-off answers: forty thousand words = "Shame the only one you ever use is 'me'."; grown men weep = onion soup; too deep = the puddle on the library steps; can't take their eyes off me = car crashes. A wrong answer ends the round; try again.
20. Win: Percival hands over **Ivy's notebook**.
21. Campus: take the frisbee from the grass and throw it at the founder statue for the sunglasses.
22. Give Ivy the notebook and the sunglasses (either order): she gives you her **bookmark**.
23. Talk to Roxy on the stage for the Talent Round. Act 2 (Greek Row) opens on the campus map.

## Act 2: Greek Week (campus map > Greek Row)
1. Greek Row: talk to Tess on the sorority porch. The frat stole Fernanda, the flamingo.
2. Talk to Chad at the frat door: toga party, no toga, no entry.
3. Talk to Tess again, choose "Tell her about the toga rule", then take the bedsheet from the clothesline.
4. Fuse Box bar: talk to Dolly (her fuse is blown), then ask her for a safety pin. Combine the sheet and the pin into a toga.
5. Give the toga to Chad. Inside the frat house, talk to Moose, the pledge guarding Fernanda. Ask about flamingo watch (he only leaves if another pledge relieves him) and about his necktie (he would trade it for food).
6. Use the breaker box to get the 30-amp fuse. The lights go out, but Moose still won't let go of Fernanda.
7. Take the basement door. Talk to Dunk (ask about the dryer and about Mother Whitlock). The blackout stopped the dryer, so take a pledge beanie out of it.
8. Upstairs, give the beanie to Moose. In the dark he thinks you're a new pledge and goes to sleep in the basement.
9. Grab Fernanda, leave through the front door and give her to Tess for the **sorority pin**.
10. Fuse Box: give the fuse to Dolly and request "Disco Volcano". Mother Whitlock, the sorority housemother, confiscated her record. Ask Dolly about the record and about how she lost to Mother Whitlock (comeback: the sugar).
11. Use the bar counter to get a free Volcano Smoothie.
12. Frat basement: give the smoothie to the sleeping Moose for his clip-on necktie.
13. Greek Row: use the necktie on the sorority door to get into the parlor. Read the manners sampler (comeback: the curtains).
14. Talk to Mother Whitlock and take the parlor test. You fail, but now everyone tells you their comeback.
15. Learn the rest of the comebacks: Tess (ask how to survive the test: the shirt) and Moose in the basement (ask if he knows Mother Whitlock: the gloves). Dunk already gave you the one about the house.
16. Take the test again: "A friend with far better taste...", "...all the luckier to be a guest...", "None for me, thank you...", "...allow me to bow instead...", "Forgive me, I was speechless...". Then choose the Disco Volcano record.
17. Give the record to Dolly for the **glow-stick bracelet**.
18. Talk to Roxy and choose Start the Quiz Round: Hank Hollister, "Ode to the Quiet Kid in the Back Row", Fernanda, a necktie, "Disco Volcano".

## Act 3: Lab Rats (campus map > Science Hall)
Rooms: science_hall (hub), lab, rooftop, science_basement (Basement stairs), faculty_lounge (Faculty lounge door).
Stuck? Use Mr. Bones in the hall: "Ask him what I should do next" gives a hint for the current state.

1. Lab: talk to Mina. Professor Nibbles is in the vents, and Professor Crumb will grill her at tomorrow's defence.
2. Roof stairs: talk to Sky. Her megaphone needs two AA batteries (and she let Nibbles out).
3. Lab: ask Mina for spare batteries. Roof: give them to Sky. The megaphone scares the pigeon off the feeder.
4. Roof: take sunflower seeds from the bird feeder. Lab: take a cardboard tube from the recycling bin. Combine them into the Nibbles Trap.
5. Lab: use the trap on the big vent. It blows straight back out: the vent is set to BLOW.
6. Hall: Basement stairs. Talk to Dot (custodian). She guards the air handler and mop cart, and feels every spill in her knees (telling her about a fake spill fails).
7. Lab: use the safety shower. Dex gets soaked, the lab floods, and Dot runs up to mop it (she now stands in the lab).
8. Basement (Dot is away): use the air handler to turn Lab 3B from BLOW to DRAW. Take a spare mop head from the mop cart.
9. Lab: use the trap on the big vent again. Nibbles climbs in. Give Nibbles to Mina for the **safety goggles**.
10. Mina: "Ask about Nibbles' future" for the retirement plan. It needs the department chair's signature (Professor Crumb).
11. Hall: try the Faculty lounge door. Crumb throws students out: he wants a lab coat and a beard of distinction.
12. Hall: take Mr. Bones' coat from the hook. Combine the lab coat with the mop head into the Professor Disguise. Use it on the Faculty lounge door.
13. Learn four comebacks (any order): Mina "Ask about Professor Crumb" (Then you're overdue); Sky "Ask about Professor Crumb" (Good. Then you can see the truth on the other side); Dot "Ask about her mop" (He's carried it further than your last three grad students) and "Ask about Professor Crumb" (So do your lectures, and nobody's cancelled those).
14. Lounge: talk to Crumb, "Challenge him to Peer Review". Answers in order: one tiny hamster > "He's carried it further..."; put anyone to sleep > "So do your lectures..."; see right through it > "Good. Then you can see the truth..."; never once been wrong > "Then you're overdue." A wrong answer means start again.
15. Crumb signs the retirement plan (ask him, or give it to him). Roof: give the signed plan to Sky for the **protest button**.
16. Lab: talk to Roxy for the Dare Round: foam volcano with the goggles (not the blue beaker), pin on the button and lead the chant, hum smooth jazz (not polka). Downtown opens on the campus map.

## Act 4: Off Campus (campus map > Downtown; Quickmart, Rusty Anchor, Starlite Diner, Bijou lobby)
1. Downtown: talk to Roxy (two tokens for the Date Round). She also gives hints any time ("I'm stuck. Any tips?").
2. Talk to Bree outside the diner: she needs a camera and a captain's hat. Ask about her tips to get the free pie coupon. Ask about the theatre, then how she handles hecklers (comeback: "the back row finally gets their money's worth"). Also wish her good luck ("break a leg").
3. Quickmart: talk to Lou. Two crossing clues: 17 Across, sailor's word for the bathroom; 19 Down, the Starlite's Mystery Pie. Ask how she deals with rude customers (comeback: "nobody'll see you cry").
4. Rusty Anchor: talk to Gus. No sailor talk until he hears B-7, and his last quarter is gone. Take the fishing net from the junk barrel.
5. Downtown: use the net on the fountain for a quarter. Rusty Anchor: put the quarter in the jukebox and play B-7, "The Barnacle Waltz".
6. Talk to Gus: ask about Lou's clue (you get the HEAD coaster), ask to borrow his hat (no: his bald spot needs covering), and ask if anyone ever insulted him (comeback: "round the world in a boot").
7. Quickmart: give the coaster to Lou. HEAD fits; 19 Down still needs the pie's name.
8. Starlite Diner: talk to Sal. Ask about the Mystery Pie (the recipe is in the tin and he never leaves the grill, except to fetch coupon pie from the walk-in) and about rude customers (comeback: "still got more fans than you").
9. Give Sal the pie coupon. While he's in the walk-in freezer, use the recipe tin: the secret is APPLE. Sal comes back and gives you a slice of Mystery Pie.
10. Quickmart: talk to Lou and choose "Nineteen Down is APPLE." She smiles and gives you the **finished crossword** and the disposable camera.
11. Bijou lobby (use the Bijou Theatre downtown): talk to Mortimer. Nobody crosses the velvet rope without surviving the Heckle, and he won't heckle without Starlite pie. Give him the pie.
12. Challenge Mortimer to the Heckle. Shirt too loud = "Then the back row finally gets their money's worth!"; wet sock = "this sock's been round the world in a boot!"; dusty like the window pie = "it's still got more fans than you."; nobody's watching = "Then nobody'll see you cry." A wrong answer ends the round; try again.
13. The rope comes down. Use the costume trunk and take the commodore's wig (the gorilla suit and crown are jokes).
14. Rusty Anchor: give the wig to Gus. Commodore Gus gives you his captain's hat.
15. Give the hat to Bree. With the camera in hand, she poses right away and the camera fills up.
16. Quickmart: give the full camera to Lou (or use it on the 1-Hour Photo) for the headshots.
17. Give the headshots to Bree for the **diner name tag**.
18. Talk to Roxy and choose "I'm ready for the Date Round!": the midnight classic at the Bijou, the fluffiest slippers, "Break a leg tomorrow...". Wrong answers can be retried.

## Act 5: Uptown (campus map > Uptown)
Rooms: Uptown street, Gallery Pierre, Club Velour, Cafe Snoot (archway at the far right of the street), Velour backstage (black door left of the club stage).

1. Street: talk to Mimi (stuck in an invisible box; Pierre in the gallery has her key). Talk to Brutus (list or VIP pass only; Pierre has the passes).
2. Gallery: talk to Pierre (he fears "Nothing No. 5" is upside down). Look at the Blank canvas: the tiny signature is upside down.
3. Talk to Pierre: "Nothing No. 5 really is upside down" gets the **VIP pass**. Also ask about the key on the plinth (he wants "greater genius") and "Ask about art" (comeback 1: "A puddle is just an ocean that's practising").
4. Use the Art supplies cart twice: **paint set**, then a **paint smock**.
5. Street: Mimi, "Ask if Pierre is a snob": her beret flies onto Dex's head (**Mimi's spare beret**). Mimi, "Ask what people say about her act" (comeback 2: "Anyone could have. Nobody did").
6. Combine the beret and the smock: **Tortured Artist Disguise**.
7. Give the VIP pass to Brutus (or the Club Velour door) to get in. Brutus, "Ask about the rules" (comeback 3: "Rules are velvet ropes. They only work if you believe in them").
8. Club: talk to Jett Vex (wants to paint Pierre's canvas). Jett, "Ask how she handles critics" (comeback 4: "Noise is just music nobody's invited to dinner yet"). Roxy explains the Finals.
9. Gallery: Pierre, "Jett Vex wants to paint on your blank canvas": he hands over **Nothing No. 5**.
10. Club: give Jett the canvas and the paint set. She paints **Something No. 1**, gives it to Dex, and asks him to show it to the critic Vivian Quill at Cafe Snoot first.
11. Club: go through the Backstage door. Talk to Grizz the roadie (Brutus confiscated his knitting needles). Ask what he's knitting (a scarf for Brutus) and "Ask about touring" (comeback 5: "You haven't seen it all. You've just seen it all so far").
12. Street: Brutus, "Ask about the confiscation bin", then "Tell him what Grizz is knitting": he gives up the **knitting needles**.
13. Backstage: give the needles to Grizz: he gives you Jett's **rider croissant** (the croissant under the dome disappears).
14. Cafe Snoot: Anton the waiter blocks Vivian. Ask Anton about the pigeon (a croissant on the terrace brings down every pigeon uptown).
15. Give the croissant to Mr. Pemberton the pigeon: the flock descends, Anton runs inside, and Vivian is open (Anton and Pemberton disappear).
16. Vivian snubs florals. Use the Tortured Artist Disguise on her.
17. Vivian, "Challenge her to an art debate": the Critique-Off. Answer: noise > "Noise is just music..."; puddle > "A puddle is just an ocean..."; anyone could > "Anyone could have. Nobody did."; real gallery > "Rules are velvet ropes..."; seen it all > "You've just seen it all so far." Any wrong answer: dismissed, retry from the start.
18. Show Vivian Something No. 1: she writes **Vivian's Rave Review** (five stars).
19. Club: talk to Jett (her phone explodes with the review): **guitar pick** (token).
20. Gallery: give Pierre the painting (or the review) with both in hand: **invisible key**. Both are hung on the wall.
21. Street: give the key to Mimi: **white glove** (token).
22. Club: talk to Roxy (or use a token on her) for the Finals. Honest answers win: "I show up with what people need", "everybody's scared of something", "I'm nervous. I'm still doing it anyway." Runs `<<act 6>>`.

Stuck? Use the Jett Vex poster on the street, or ask Roxy for a tip: Dex says what to try next.

## Finale: Speed Daters Live (campus map > TV studio)

Rooms: tv_studio, tv_green_room (door under the booth window), tv_control_booth (CREW ONLY stairs in the green room).

1. Studio: Chet spills grape soda on Dex. Talk to Roxy: she needs a mic, a clean shirt, a dance-off track and proof Chet is cheating.
2. Talk to Chet (he keeps saying "copy that"). Use the studio camera: take Chet's walkie (its screen says RECEIVING FROM: BOOTH).
3. Give the walkie to Roxy. She explains the Trash-Talk Teaser: cut Chet's whisperer, then beat him at trading insults.
4. Audience > Bree: ask about the stain for the napkin and the tip. Audience > Lou: ask for club soda. Combine soda + napkin (stain gone, shirt soaking wet).
5. Sound board: eject Chet's tape from deck two (it comes out tangled). Audience > Dolly: ask her to mix on it; she says wind it back with a pencil.
6. Audience > Bree: borrow the pencil from her bun. Combine pencil + Chet's tape to wind it back in.
7. Green room: Gloria won't hand over mic packs before makeup, and the chair is reserved for Chet. Take the card off the Reserved sign.
8. Use the makeup chair: Gloria does Dex's makeup. Use the chair again (or ask Gloria) and her hair dryer dries the shirt.
9. Wardrobe rack: take a dead mic pack. Talk to Gloria and ask about the director (Norma is scared of rodents).
10. Booth stairs: press TALK. Norma only lets crew in (headset + lanyard) and is waiting for a runner, Kevin.
11. Craft services: take a bendy straw and a cheese puff.
12. Audience > Tess: ask about a runner called Kevin for Kevin's lanyard. Audience > Dolly: borrow her headphones.
13. Combine headphones + bendy straw = crew headset; combine headset + lanyard = the Kevin Kit.
14. Audience > Mina: ask for a battery for the mic (Sky has it). Audience > Sky: she trades the battery for a protest sign.
15. Green room > Gloria: borrow a lipstick (Scandal Red). Combine the RESERVED card + lipstick = CHET IS A CHEAT sign.
16. Give the sign to the audience (Sky) for the nine-volt. Combine mic pack + nine-volt.
17. Booth stairs with the Kevin Kit: Norma lets "Kevin" up. Look at or use the playback deck: Norma guards Chet's tape.
18. Audience > Mina: ask if Nibbles can help (he needs a cheese puff); give her the cheese puff for Professor Nibbles.
19. Booth: use Nibbles on Norma (or the deck). She jumps on her chair. Use the playback deck: take Chet's Comebacks tape.
20. Studio: give the Kevin Kit to the audience (Dolly gets her headphones back). Give Chet's wound tape to the audience: Dolly records Floor-Fillers.
21. Put Dolly's tape in the sound board.
22. Learn comebacks (audience, "Chet's going to trash-talk me"): Lacey, Lou, Ivy, Tess, Jett. (Gloria's "roots" line is a red herring.)
23. Talk to Chet > Trash talk. Answers in order:
    folding chair -> "A folding chair still holds folks up..."; teeth -> "Nice teeth. Shame nothing smart...";
    trophy cabinet -> "Must be quiet in there..."; everybody came to see me -> "Funny. Tess made the seating chart...";
    nobody will remember your name -> "Sorry, what was your name again? Chad? Chip? Chuck?". A wrong answer just restarts it.
24. Chet blurts "my tape!"; Dex hands Roxy the cheat tape and Chet is busted.
25. Talk to Roxy > "We're ready" (or stand on the contestant mark). Show: token callbacks, Uncle Vic's motto
    ("Be the guy who shows up with what they need"), dance-off: scoot like Lacey, invisible box, air-guitar solo. THE END.
